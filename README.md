# Groundwater Quality Assessment and Spatial Distribution of Pollutants Using GIS

This repository presents a GIS-based conceptual case study focused on groundwater quality assessment and spatial visualization of pollutant distribution in the area south of the industrial zone of Pančevo, Serbia.

The project demonstrates how GIS can support environmental monitoring workflows through the integration of sampling locations, laboratory-style datasets, spatial interpretation, thematic mapping, and project organization principles.

## Project Overview

The study area includes multiple piezometer locations positioned near industrial and transport-related zones. The workflow was developed to demonstrate how groundwater contamination patterns may be explored using geospatial analysis techniques within QGIS.

The project focuses on:

* groundwater monitoring workflows,
* spatial visualization of pollutant concentrations,
* interpretation of potential contamination patterns,
* identification of locations requiring additional monitoring attention,
* and GIS-based environmental project organization.

## Project Management Context

This repository was also developed as part of a GIS Project Management case study following PMI-oriented project organization principles, including:

* workflow planning,
* documentation structure,
* field coordination,
* environmental data management,
* and spatial analysis organization.

## Methodology

The workflow included:

* organization of piezometer sampling locations,
* preparation of environmental attribute tables,
* GIS-based spatial visualization in QGIS,
* thematic mapping of selected parameters,
* spatial interpretation of concentration gradients,
* and identification of potential hotspot locations.

## Parameters Included

The conceptual dataset includes selected environmental indicators commonly used in groundwater quality assessment, including:

* heavy metals,
* Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC),
* and physico-chemical groundwater parameters.

## Data Disclaimer

The concentration values included in this repository are simulated and used exclusively for educational, analytical, and portfolio demonstration purposes.

This repository does not represent an official environmental assessment or regulatory investigation.
